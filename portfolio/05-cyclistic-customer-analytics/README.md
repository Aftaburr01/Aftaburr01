# Cyclistic Customer Analytics

## Business Problem

How can customer behavior data be used to understand differences between **member and casual riders** and translate those differences into practical marketing and retention opportunities?

This project applies a repeatable Python/pandas workflow to customer behavior analysis, data preparation, segmentation, and business recommendation development.

## Analytical Focus

The analysis emphasizes:

- customer-type behavior
- ride-pattern differences
- data cleaning and preparation
- repeatable analysis workflows
- segmentation and business interpretation
- translating behavioral patterns into actionable recommendations

## Key Business Insight

The analysis identifies a meaningful behavioral distinction between the two customer groups:

- **Members** tend to exhibit more weekday and commute-oriented usage patterns.
- **Casual riders** show stronger weekend and seasonal usage patterns.

These differences provide a basis for targeted marketing rather than treating all riders as one customer population.

## Recommendations

The project recommends testing strategies such as:

1. **Weekend-to-weekday conversion messaging** to encourage casual riders to experience member-style weekday value.
2. **Seasonal campaigns** aligned with periods of higher casual-rider activity.
3. **Commute-framed messaging** during relevant afternoon and weekday periods.
4. Measurement of conversion and retention outcomes through controlled campaign testing.
