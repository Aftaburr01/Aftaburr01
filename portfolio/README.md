# Professional Analytics Portfolio

This portfolio presents selected data analytics work through a **business-first lens**: define the operational problem, analyze the data, evaluate the evidence, and translate findings into practical decisions.

## Portfolio focus

- **Operations & Supply Chain Analytics** — inventory, transportation, fulfillment, and service performance
- **Business Intelligence** — KPI development, dashboards, trend analysis, and stakeholder-ready recommendations
- **Predictive Analytics** — supervised machine learning and model evaluation
- **Customer & Retail Analytics** — segmentation, purchasing behavior, and market-basket analysis

## Featured projects

### 1. Food Delivery Time Prediction
Predictive analytics applied to delivery-time performance. The project combines data-quality review, geographic feature engineering, exploratory analysis, and model comparison to identify practical levers for improving ETA accuracy and delivery operations.

**Methods:** Python, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, feature engineering, Haversine distance, regression, model evaluation

### 2. Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization
A supply-chain analytics project focused on inventory investment, service levels, lead-time variability, ABC/XYZ segmentation, safety stock, EOQ, supplier performance, and working-capital opportunities.

**Methods:** Python, pandas, inventory analytics, ABC/XYZ analysis, EOQ, stochastic safety stock, supplier/SLA analysis

### 3. Retail Market Basket Analysis
Association-rule analysis used to identify products that are frequently purchased together and translate those relationships into merchandising, bundling, and promotional opportunities.

**Methods:** Python, pandas, Apriori, association rules, support, confidence, lift

## How I approach analytics

1. **Start with the business question.**
2. **Validate the data before modeling.**
3. **Use analysis to explain what is happening and why.**
4. **Use predictive methods when they add decision value.**
5. **Translate results into measurable business actions.**
6. **Document assumptions, limitations, and opportunities for improvement.**
